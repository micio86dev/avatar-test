# Scope-Shift Table — LRN (Learning)

> Authored before any new LRN anchor was drafted, per
> `docs/app_description/02-domain/framework-authoring/house-voice-and-anti-hedge-standard.md`
> §4 and this directory's `README.md`. LRN is assigned to ICO, FLL, MLL, BUL
> and SRX (`roles.json`) and all four already carry authored content — but it
> predates this change and is written in the pre-standard register (e.g.
> `FLL/LRN` level 3: *"Open to feedback when offered, but rarely initiates
> requests"*). It is **not** a house-voice model here. Only the SRX row is
> new; the other four are included for the ladder's completeness, not as
> calibration.

| Role | Object | Horizon | Unit of accountability |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICO | own individual skills and knowledge, developed within own tasks | daily–weekly | own area |
| FLL | own skills and knowledge, applied to running a small area or department | weekly–monthly | small area / department |
| MLL | own skills and knowledge, applied to aligning unit plans across one or two related functions within a single country | ~1 year | small country |
| BUL | own skills and knowledge, applied to executing organizational strategy across multiple business functions in a country or region | 1–2 years | country or region |
| SRX | own skills and knowledge, applied to setting organizational direction across business units and multi-country operations | 3–5 years | whole organization / multi-country |

**Rejection check** — every row read against every other: the object stays
"own skills and knowledge" for every role by design (the competency is about
self-development, not delegated to others — that is INS's object, not
LRN's), so the differentiator is deliberately the **application** each role's
own accountability puts them to: ICO applies them within its own tasks; FLL
to running a department; MLL to a second function's worth of coordination in
one country; BUL to executing strategy across multiple functions in a
country or region; SRX to setting direction across business units and
countries. No two rows collapse once each role's own accountability unit
(per `roles.json`) is named. Table passes.

**Source**: every "Object" cell's application clause is drawn from each
role's `responsibilities` field in `roles.json` — ICO "executing tasks...
within their own area"; FLL "coordinating the execution of operational
goals... within a small area or department"; MLL "aligning unit plans with
organizational strategy... within a small country"; BUL "executing
organizational strategy... across an entire country or region"; SRX "setting
organizational direction and long-term strategy... across the entire
organization or a multi-country region." "Horizon" and "Unit of
accountability" are fixed by the ladder (house-voice standard §3).
