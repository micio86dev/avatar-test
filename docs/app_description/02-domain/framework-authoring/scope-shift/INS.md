# Scope-Shift Table — INS (Inspiring Others)

> Authored before any new INS anchor was drafted, per
> `docs/app_description/02-domain/framework-authoring/house-voice-and-anti-hedge-standard.md`
> §4 and this directory's `README.md`. ICO does not carry INS (`roles.json`),
> so this table has four rows, not five. FLL, MLL, BUL and SRX all carry INS,
> and FLL/MLL/BUL already have authored content — but it predates this
> change and is written in the pre-standard register (e.g. `MLL/INS` level 3:
> *"Provides occasional feedback to help others reflect on their
> performance"*). It is **not** a house-voice model here. Only the SRX row is
> new; the other three are included for the ladder's completeness, not as
> calibration.

| Role | Object | Horizon | Unit of accountability |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLL | the growth and performance of individual members of a small area or department | weekly–monthly | small area / department |
| MLL | the growth and performance of people across one or two related functions within a single country | ~1 year | small country |
| BUL | the development of leaders and performance culture across multiple business functions in a country or region | 1–2 years | country or region |
| SRX | the development of other senior leaders and the performance culture across business units and multi-country operations | 3–5 years | whole organization / multi-country |

**Rejection check** — every row read against every other: FLL develops
individual members of one department; MLL's object crosses into a second
function within one country; BUL's object shifts to developing **leaders**
and a performance culture across multiple functions; SRX is the only row
developing **other senior leaders themselves** — peers in seniority, not
subordinates — across business units and countries. No two rows collapse
once each role's own accountability unit (per `roles.json`) is named. Table
passes.

**Source**: every "Object" cell is drawn from each role's `responsibilities`
field in `roles.json` — FLL "overseeing one functional unit" within "a small
area or department"; MLL "managing one or two closely related functions"
within "a small country"; BUL "overseeing multiple business functions"
across "an entire country or region"; SRX "leading other senior leaders
across all business functions" spanning "the entire organization or a
multi-country region." "Horizon" and "Unit of accountability" are fixed by
the ladder (house-voice standard §3).
