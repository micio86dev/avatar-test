# Scope-Shift Table — STG (Strategy)

> Authored before any new STG anchor was drafted, per
> `docs/app_description/02-domain/framework-authoring/house-voice-and-anti-hedge-standard.md`
> §4 and this directory's `README.md`. STG is assigned to ICO, FLL, MLL, BUL
> and SRX (`roles.json`) and all four already carry authored content — but
> that content predates this change and is written in the pre-standard
> register the house-voice document's §Scope explicitly does not retro-review:
> third-person `-s` verb forms, terminal periods, and level triples that
> often differ only by a hedge adverb (`FLL/STG` level 3: *"Creates basic
> plans to achieve team goals but may lack clarity or follow-through"*). It is
> **not** a house-voice model and is **not** read as one here. Only the SRX
> row below is new content under this change; the other four rows are
> included, unmodified, for the ladder's completeness and as the reviewer's
> reference for what the role already owns — not as calibration for the new
> anchors' voice.

| Role | Object | Horizon | Unit of accountability |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICO | how own daily tasks connect to team and organizational goals | daily–weekly | own area |
| FLL | operational plans for a small area or department that stay aligned with organizational direction | weekly–monthly | small area / department |
| MLL | unit plans across one or two related functions that stay aligned with organizational direction, within a single country | ~1 year | small country |
| BUL | organizational direction carried out across multiple business functions in a country or region, weighed against full P&L | 1–2 years | country or region |
| SRX | organizational direction spanning business units and multi-country operations | 3–5 years | whole organization / multi-country |

**Rejection check** — every row read against every other: ICO connects its own
tasks to goals it does not set; FLL keeps a department's plans aligned with a
direction it also does not set; MLL adds a second function and a country's
worth of coordination; BUL adds full-P&L weight and multiple functions but is
still executing a direction set above it; SRX is the only row that **sets**
that direction rather than aligning to it, across business units and
countries. No two rows collapse to the same object once each role's own
accountability unit (per `roles.json`) is named. Table passes.

**Source**: every "Object" cell is drawn from each role's `responsibilities`
field in `roles.json` — ICO "contributing to functional goals... within their
own area"; FLL "coordinating the execution of operational goals... within a
small area or department"; MLL "aligning unit plans with organizational
strategy... within a small country... one or two closely related functions";
BUL "executing organizational strategy... full P&L... multiple business
functions"; SRX "setting organizational direction and long-term strategy...
across the entire organization or a multi-country region." "Horizon" and
"Unit of accountability" are fixed by the ladder (house-voice standard §3).
