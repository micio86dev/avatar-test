# Scope-Shift Table — RES (Resilience)

> Authored before any new RES anchor was drafted, per
> `docs/app_description/02-domain/framework-authoring/house-voice-and-anti-hedge-standard.md`
> §4 and this directory's `README.md`. RES is assigned to ICO, FLL, MLL, BUL
> and SRX (`roles.json`) and all four already carry authored content — but it
> predates this change and is written in the pre-standard register (this is
> one of the two competencies design.md §7 names as already carrying a
> generated cross-role duplicate baseline entry, `FLL.json:176` ==
> `MLL.json:176` — a defect this change records rather than fixes,
> per that document's own §Scope). It is **not** a house-voice model here.
> Only the SRX row is new; the other four are included for the ladder's
> completeness, not as calibration.

| Role | Object | Horizon | Unit of accountability |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICO | own composure and output under day-to-day uncertainty within own tasks | daily–weekly | own area |
| FLL | own area's composure and output when priorities shift week to week | weekly–monthly | small area / department |
| MLL | unit-level composure and output when priorities shift across related functions over roughly a year | ~1 year | small country |
| BUL | composure and output across multiple business functions in a country or region under sustained 1–2 year pressure | 1–2 years | country or region |
| SRX | organizational composure and output across business units and multi-country operations under sustained multi-year uncertainty | 3–5 years | whole organization / multi-country |

**Rejection check** — every row read against every other: ICO holds its own
composure within its own tasks day to day; FLL holds an area's composure
week to week; MLL's object crosses into related functions over close to a
year; BUL's object is composure across multiple functions under 1–2 years of
P&L pressure; SRX's object is organizational composure across business units
and countries under sustained multi-year uncertainty, the widest and longest
scope of the five. No two rows collapse once each role's own accountability
unit (per `roles.json`) is named. Table passes.

**Source**: every "Object" cell is drawn from each role's `responsibilities`
field in `roles.json`, cross-referenced with each role's own horizon —
ICO "short-term focus (daily to weekly)"; FLL "medium-term focus (weekly to
monthly)"; MLL "long-term focus (1 year)"; BUL "long-term focus (1-2
years)... multiple business functions"; SRX "multi-year focus (3-5 years)...
across the entire organization or a multi-country region." "Horizon" and
"Unit of accountability" are fixed by the ladder (house-voice standard §3).
