# Scope-Shift Table — INC (Inclusion)

> Authored before any new INC anchor was drafted, per
> `docs/app_description/02-domain/framework-authoring/house-voice-and-anti-hedge-standard.md`
> §4 and this directory's `README.md`. ICO carries INC and already has
> authored content (part of the existing 15 ICO pairs); its row is included
> for the ladder's completeness and as the reviewer's baseline, not as new
> content in this change. FLL/MLL/SRX rows are new. BUL does not carry INC
> (`roles.json`), so this table has no BUL row.

| Role | Object | Horizon | Unit of accountability |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICO | own interactions with different colleagues within own area | daily–weekly | own area |
| FLL | inclusion practices applied within a small area or department's team | weekly–monthly | small area / department |
| MLL | inclusion practices applied across own country's functions | ~1 year | small country |
| SRX | inclusion practices and policy applied across the organization | 3–5 years | whole organization / multi-country |

**Rejection check** — every row read against every other: ICO's object is a
peer-to-peer interaction with no policy-setting authority; FLL's object adds
a PRACTICE applied to a team FLL manages, a dimension ICO's peer-only scope
lacks; MLL's object is a practice applied across MULTIPLE functions, beyond
FLL's single-team reach; SRX's object is organization-wide POLICY, beyond
MLL's single-country reach. No two rows collapse to the same object once each
role's own accountability unit (per `roles.json`) is named. Table passes.

**Source**: every "Object" cell is drawn from each role's `responsibilities`
field in `roles.json` — ICO "executing tasks... within their own area...
without direct managerial duties"; FLL "within a small area or department";
MLL "managing one or two closely related functions" within "a small
country"; SRX "across the entire organization or a multi-country region."
"Horizon" and "Unit of accountability" are fixed by the ladder (house-voice
standard §3).
