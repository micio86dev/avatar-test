# Scope-Shift Table — TMG (Team Management)

> Authored before any TMG anchor was drafted, per
> `docs/app_description/02-domain/framework-authoring/house-voice-and-anti-hedge-standard.md`
> §4 and this directory's `README.md`. ICO does not carry TMG
> (`roles.json`), so this table has four rows, not five.

| Role | Object | Horizon | Unit of accountability |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLL | goal-setting, delegation, and feedback for individual team members within one functional unit | weekly–monthly | small area / department |
| MLL | goal-setting, delegation, and feedback for team leads across one or two related functions | ~1 year | small country |
| BUL | goal-setting, delegation, and feedback for functional leaders across multiple business functions | 1–2 years | country or region |
| SRX | goal-setting, delegation, and feedback for other senior leaders across all business functions | 3–5 years | whole organization / multi-country |

**Rejection check** — every row read against every other: the object of
management climbs the ladder itself — FLL manages individual contributors,
MLL and BUL manage people who themselves manage (team leads, then functional
leaders), and SRX manages other senior leaders. This is not the same
behaviour with the role name swapped: who is being led is a genuinely
different population at each rung, drawn from `roles.json`'s own
`competencies` assignment (only ICO is excluded from TMG) and each role's
`responsibilities` scope. Table passes.

**Source**: "Object" cells trace to each role's `responsibilities` field —
FLL "overseeing one functional unit"; MLL "managing one or two closely
related functions"; BUL "overseeing multiple business functions"; SRX
"leading other senior leaders across all business functions." "Horizon" and
"Unit of accountability" are fixed by the ladder (house-voice standard §3).
