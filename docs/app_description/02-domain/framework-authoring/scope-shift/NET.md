# Scope-Shift Table — NET (Networking)

> Authored before any new NET anchor was drafted, per
> `docs/app_description/02-domain/framework-authoring/house-voice-and-anti-hedge-standard.md`
> §4 and this directory's `README.md`. ICO carries NET and already has
> authored content (part of the existing 15 ICO pairs); its row is included
> for the ladder's completeness and as the reviewer's baseline, not as new
> content in this change. FLL/MLL/BUL/SRX rows are new.

| Role | Object | Horizon | Unit of accountability |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICO | relationships with peers reached day-to-day within own area | daily–weekly | own area |
| FLL | relationships with peers and stakeholders reached within a small area or department | weekly–monthly | small area / department |
| MLL | relationships with peers and stakeholders across own country's functions | ~1 year | small country |
| BUL | relationships with peers and external stakeholders across a country or region | 1–2 years | country or region |
| SRX | relationships with industry contacts and external stakeholders across the organization | 3–5 years | whole organization / multi-country |

**Rejection check** — every row read against every other: ICO's object stays
inside one area, entirely internal; FLL's object still stays internal but
reaches stakeholders beyond ICO's single area; MLL's object crosses into
other functions within one country, beyond FLL's single-department reach;
BUL's object explicitly adds EXTERNAL stakeholders, absent from the first
three; SRX's object adds INDUSTRY contacts at an organization-wide scale,
beyond BUL's single-region reach. No two rows collapse to the same object
once each role's own accountability unit (per `roles.json`) is named. Table
passes.

**Source**: every "Object" cell is drawn from each role's `responsibilities`
field in `roles.json` — ICO "executing tasks... within their own area";
FLL "within a small area or department... overseeing one functional unit";
MLL "managing one or two closely related functions" within "a small
country"; BUL "across an entire country or region"; SRX "across the entire
organization or a multi-country region." "Horizon" and "Unit of
accountability" are fixed by the ladder (house-voice standard §3).
