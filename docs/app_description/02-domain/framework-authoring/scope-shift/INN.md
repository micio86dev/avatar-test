# Scope-Shift Table — INN (Innovation)

> Authored before any new INN anchor was drafted, per
> `docs/app_description/02-domain/framework-authoring/house-voice-and-anti-hedge-standard.md`
> §4 and this directory's `README.md`. INN is assigned to ICO, FLL, MLL, BUL
> and SRX (`roles.json`) and all four already carry authored content — but,
> as with every competency in this batch, it predates this change and is
> written in the pre-standard register (e.g. `FLL/INN` level 3: *"Occasionally
> promotes alternative thinking but without consistent follow-up"* — a hedge
> adverb bolted onto level 5's sentence, exactly the shape
> house-voice-and-anti-hedge-standard.md §2.1 exists to reject in new
> content). It is **not** a house-voice model here. Only the SRX row is new;
> the other four are included for the ladder's completeness, not as
> calibration.

| Role | Object | Horizon | Unit of accountability |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICO | generating fresh ideas and questioning current methods within own individual tasks | daily–weekly | own area |
| FLL | championing process or method improvements within a small area or department | weekly–monthly | small area / department |
| MLL | championing improvements across one or two related functions within a single country | ~1 year | small country |
| BUL | championing innovation initiatives across multiple business functions in a country or region | 1–2 years | country or region |
| SRX | championing innovation and new ventures across business units and multi-country operations, backing the leaders who drive them | 3–5 years | whole organization / multi-country |

**Rejection check** — every row read against every other: ICO generates ideas
within its own tasks and questions nothing beyond them; FLL champions
improvements a department can adopt on its own; MLL's object crosses into a
second function within one country; BUL's championing reaches multiple
functions and a whole country or region; SRX is the only row whose object
includes backing OTHER leaders who take the risk, spanning business units and
countries a single P&L never reaches. No two rows collapse once each role's
own accountability unit (per `roles.json`) is named. Table passes.

**Source**: every "Object" cell is drawn from each role's `responsibilities`
field in `roles.json` — ICO "executing tasks... within their own area";
FLL "coordinating the execution of operational goals... within a small area
or department"; MLL "managing one or two closely related functions" within
"a small country"; BUL "overseeing multiple business functions" across
"an entire country or region"; SRX "leading other senior leaders across all
business functions" spanning "the entire organization or a multi-country
region." "Horizon" and "Unit of accountability" are fixed by the ladder
(house-voice standard §3).
