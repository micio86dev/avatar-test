# Scope-Shift Table — COL (Collaboration)

> Authored before any new COL anchor was drafted, per
> `docs/app_description/02-domain/framework-authoring/house-voice-and-anti-hedge-standard.md`
> §4 and this directory's `README.md`. ICO carries COL and already has
> authored content (part of the existing 15 ICO pairs); its row is included
> for the ladder's completeness and as the reviewer's baseline, not as new
> content in this change. FLL/MLL/BUL/SRX rows are new.

| Role | Object | Horizon | Unit of accountability |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICO | joint effort with peers on shared individual-contributor tasks | daily–weekly | own area |
| FLL | joint effort and workload-sharing among own team's members within one functional unit | weekly–monthly | small area / department |
| MLL | joint effort and shared decisions between team leads across own one or two functions | ~1 year | small country |
| BUL | joint effort and shared decisions between functional leaders across multiple business functions | 1–2 years | country or region |
| SRX | joint effort and shared decisions between business unit leaders across the organization | 3–5 years | whole organization / multi-country |

**Rejection check** — every row read against every other: ICO's object is
peer-to-peer task collaboration with no delegation dimension at all; FLL's
object adds workload-sharing across a team FLL itself manages, which ICO's
peer-only scope never reaches; MLL's object is collaboration between team
LEADS across functions, a population ICO and FLL do not manage; BUL's object
is collaboration between functional LEADERS across multiple functions, a rung
above MLL's team leads; SRX's object is collaboration between business unit
LEADERS at the organization level, a rung above BUL's functional leaders. The
object climbs the leadership ladder itself at every row — never the same
population with the role name swapped. Table passes.

**Source**: every "Object" cell is drawn from each role's `responsibilities`
field in `roles.json` — ICO "executing tasks... within their own area...
without direct managerial duties"; FLL "overseeing one functional unit"; MLL
"managing one or two closely related functions"; BUL "overseeing multiple
business functions"; SRX "leading other senior leaders across all business
functions." "Horizon" and "Unit of accountability" are fixed by the ladder
(house-voice standard §3).
