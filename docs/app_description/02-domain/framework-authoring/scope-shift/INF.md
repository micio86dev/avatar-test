# Scope-Shift Table — INF (Influence)

> Authored before any new INF anchor was drafted, per
> `docs/app_description/02-domain/framework-authoring/house-voice-and-anti-hedge-standard.md`
> §4 and this directory's `README.md`. INF is assigned to ICO, FLL, MLL, BUL
> and SRX (`roles.json`) and all four already carry authored content — but it
> predates this change and is written in the pre-standard register (e.g.
> `BUL/INF` level 3: *"Generally considers others' views but may lack
> flexibility in discussion"*). It is **not** a house-voice model here. Only
> the SRX row is new; the other four are included for the ladder's
> completeness, not as calibration.

| Role | Object | Horizon | Unit of accountability |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICO | own positions and proposals put to a small circle of immediate colleagues | daily–weekly | own area |
| FLL | proposals and decisions put to a small area or department | weekly–monthly | small area / department |
| MLL | proposals and decisions put to stakeholders across one or two related functions within a single country | ~1 year | small country |
| BUL | strategic proposals put to stakeholders across multiple business functions in a country or region | 1–2 years | country or region |
| SRX | organizational direction and capital allocation decisions put to business unit leaders across the organization or a multi-country region | 3–5 years | whole organization / multi-country |

**Rejection check** — every row read against every other: ICO argues for its
own positions with immediate colleagues; FLL puts proposals to one area or
department; MLL's audience crosses into a second function within one
country; BUL's proposals are explicitly strategic and reach multiple
functions in a country or region; SRX is the only row whose object is
capital allocation decisions put to **business unit leaders** across the
whole organization or a multi-country region. No two rows collapse once each
role's own accountability unit (per `roles.json`) is named. Table passes.

**Source**: every "Object" cell is drawn from each role's `responsibilities`
field in `roles.json` — ICO "within their own area"; FLL "within a small
area or department"; MLL "managing one or two closely related functions"
within "a small country"; BUL "handling full P&L responsibilities...
overseeing multiple business functions"; SRX "owning capital allocation and
consolidated P&L across business units" and "leading other senior leaders."
"Horizon" and "Unit of accountability" are fixed by the ladder (house-voice
standard §3).
