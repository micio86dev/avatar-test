# Scope-Shift Table — COM (Communication)

> Authored before any new COM anchor was drafted, per
> `docs/app_description/02-domain/framework-authoring/house-voice-and-anti-hedge-standard.md`
> §4 and this directory's `README.md`. ICO carries COM and already has
> authored content (part of the existing 15 ICO pairs); its row is included
> for the ladder's completeness and as the reviewer's baseline, not as new
> content in this change. FLL/MLL/SRX rows are new. BUL does not carry COM
> (`roles.json`), so this table has no BUL row.

| Role | Object | Horizon | Unit of accountability |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICO | own updates and ideas shared with peers, with no team to inform | daily–weekly | own area |
| FLL | expectations and updates communicated within a small area or department | weekly–monthly | small area / department |
| MLL | plans and updates communicated across own country's functions | ~1 year | small country |
| SRX | strategy and direction communicated across the organization | 3–5 years | whole organization / multi-country |

**Rejection check** — every row read against every other: ICO's object is a
peer update with no directive content; FLL's object is EXPECTATIONS set for
a team FLL manages, a dimension ICO's peer-to-peer object lacks entirely;
MLL's object is a PLAN spanning multiple functions, beyond FLL's
single-department reach; SRX's object is organization-wide STRATEGY, beyond
MLL's single-country reach. No two rows collapse to the same object once each
role's own accountability unit (per `roles.json`) is named. Table passes.

**Source**: every "Object" cell is drawn from each role's `responsibilities`
field in `roles.json` — ICO "executing tasks... within their own area...
without direct managerial duties"; FLL "coordinating the execution of
operational goals... within a small area or department"; MLL "aligning unit
plans with organizational strategy... managing one or two closely related
functions"; SRX "setting organizational direction and long-term strategy...
across the entire organization or a multi-country region." "Horizon" and
"Unit of accountability" are fixed by the ladder (house-voice standard §3).
